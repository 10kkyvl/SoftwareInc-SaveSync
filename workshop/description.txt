[h1]Save Sync[/h1]
[b]Got a data mod update, or found a new mod, but you are 30 years into your company? Save Sync brings the new content into the save you already have.[/b]

[list]
[*]Your original save is never changed. The result goes to a new save file.
[*]You see every change before anything happens.
[*]When two of your mods disagree, you decide.
[/list]

[hr][/hr]

[h2]How it works[/h2]
[olist]
[*]Load your save. If a mod in it has something new, a window lists what will be [b]added[/b], what will be [b]updated[/b] and what [b]cannot be applied[/b].
[*]For each mod choose [b]Add to a new save[/b], [b]Skip this mod[/b] or [b]Decide later[/b]. Skipped mods stop asking.
[*]If two mods change the same software type, pick whose numbers to use, or keep the ones in your save. One switch applies your choice to all remaining types.
[*]Save Sync writes a new save, loads it and adds the content. [b]Save the game afterwards[/b] to keep it.
[/olist]
A mod your save has never used does not pop up on its own. Add it from [i]Options > Mods > Save Sync > Check all installed mods[/i].

[hr][/hr]

[h2]What it adds[/h2]
[list]
[*]New software types, categories and features
[*]Add-ons for existing software types
[*]New company types, and new software for existing company types
[*]The name generators all of this needs
[*]Updated popularity, unlock years, hidden flag and submarkets
[*]Renamed submarkets
[/list]

[h2]What it does not do[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]It only adds.[/b] Content a mod removed or renamed stays in your save.
[*]Products already released and projects already started keep their old features.
[*]DevTime, server load, ideal price, retention and similar numbers of existing content cannot change from a workshop mod. The window lists them as "will not change".
[*][b]Hardware[/b], manufacturing and new tech specializations are not supported yet.
[*]If a mod adds a main feature whose specialization your type already uses under another name, that main feature is skipped and its sub features go under the existing one.
[*]New categories keep the default submarket split.
[*]Does nothing in multiplayer, in saves from multiplayer, or in the editor.
[/list]

[hr][/hr]

[h2]Questions[/h2]
[b]Is it safe?[/b]
Your original save file is not touched, so you can always go back to it. Still, keep a copy of your saves, as with any mod that changes a save.

[b]When do AI companies start making the new software?[/b]
Over time, as in a new game. New companies can take from a few game months to a couple of years to appear.

[b]Can I remove Save Sync later?[/b]
Yes. Saves keep working without it, and the content it added stays in the saves you saved after adding it.

[b]A window keeps asking about a mod I do not want.[/b]
Press [b]Skip this mod[/b]. [b]Decide later[/b] asks again on the next load.

[h2]Русский[/h2]
Save Sync добавляет в идущий сейв новое содержимое обновлённого или нового дата-мода: типы ПО, категории, фичи, аддоны и типы компаний. Исходный сейв не меняется, результат пишется в новый файл. Перед применением мод показывает все изменения, а при конфликте двух модов ты выбираешь, чьи числа брать. Мод только добавляет: удалённое в моде остаётся в сейве, выпущенные продукты не меняются, железо и новые специализации пока не поддерживаются. Сделай копию сейвов перед использованием.
